# WUDAPT India Atlas: Interactive Viewer

An interactive web version of the **WUDAPT India Atlas**: Local Climate Zone (LCZ) maps for 97 Indian smart cities and megacities, classified from Landsat-8 imagery with the WUDAPT protocol.

**To view it:** download `index.html` and open it in any browser.

## What you can do

- **Explore the map of India.** Click any city, or colour the cities by dominant zone, built-up share, vegetation or water.
- **Open a city page.** See area, population and rainfall, the LCZ mix for all 17 zones, the original atlas map sheet and the city description.
- **Compare all cities** side by side, sorted by built-up, vegetation, bare ground, water, heavy industry or number of zones.
- **Read the 17 zone definitions** (Stewart and Oke, 2012).

## Data and method

- LCZ maps, city profiles and charts are from the *WUDAPT India Atlas* (Safaee, Deshmukh and Niyogi, Department of Agronomy, Purdue University).
- Zone shares and areas are taken from the atlas charts.
- The India outline is the DataMeet composite boundary.
